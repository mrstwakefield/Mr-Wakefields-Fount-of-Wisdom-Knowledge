Class progress

Paste the progress report codes your students send you (from Trophies → "Send your progress to your teacher"). Everything is decoded here in your browser: nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere. Codes are self-reported, so treat them as a guide.

Paste codes

If two codes have the same name, the newest one is used. "💤" marks students with no revision in the last 7 days of their report, or whose report is more than 14 days old.
